06 / Buyer answers

Ophira Resort Residences FAQ

Clear answers to the questions buyers ask first.

Ask the sales team

Where is Ophira Resort Residences located?

Ophira Resort Residences is in Dutamas, Kuala Lumpur, approximately 400 m from a KTM commuter rail connection.

Is Ophira a freehold development?

Yes. Ophira is a freehold, GreenRE-certified serviced apartment development on commercial-titled land.

Who is the developer?

Ophira Resort Residences is developed by Chin Hin Property Development, part of Chin Hin Group.

What home sizes are available?

Layouts range from 525 to 1,020 sq. ft., with 1+1, 2+1, 3- and 4-bedroom configurations. Selected C1, D1 and D2 layouts are dual-key enabled.

How many facilities are planned?

There are 39 facilities across Level 1, Level 10 and the sky garden, including a 50 m lap pool, gymnasium, co-working space, pickleball courts and a social lounge.

How much parking is provided?

Homes receive one to three bays depending on type. The development also plans 82 visitor bays, 12 EV bays and two car-wash bays.

What is the estimated maintenance fee?

Approximately RM0.36 per sq. ft. per month, including sinking fund. Actual charges depend on allocated share units.

When is completion expected?

The current target is Q4 2031, with an indicated 62-month construction period. Dates remain subject to the SPA and project approvals.

Are pets allowed?

Pets are permitted subject to the applicable strata by-laws, local regulations and nuisance restrictions.

What is included in selected homes?

Selected homes include a fitted kitchen with hood and hob, smart-home system, instant water heaters and inverter air-conditioners. Inclusions vary by type and package.
