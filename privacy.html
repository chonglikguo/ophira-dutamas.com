Privacy notice

Your enquiry details stay focused on your request.

Last updated 2 October 2026

This notice explains how information submitted through the Ophira Resort Residences enquiry form is used. It applies to this website at ophira-dutamas.com.

Information collected

The form may collect your name, WhatsApp-enabled contact phone number, email address, preferred layout and any message you choose to provide.

How the information is used

Your details are used to respond to your enquiry, provide requested project information, arrange viewings and recommend suitable layouts. The submission may be recorded in a sales lead register and forwarded to authorised sales channels.

Sharing and retention

Information is shared only with parties responsible for responding to Ophira Resort Residences enquiries and the service providers used to process the form. Records are retained only as long as reasonably needed for the enquiry, sales follow-up and applicable legal obligations.

Your choices

You may ask the sales team to correct your details or stop follow-up communication by replying to an email received in response to your enquiry.

External services

The enquiry form uses a Google Apps Script endpoint to route submissions to authorised email, Google Sheets and Telegram recipients.
